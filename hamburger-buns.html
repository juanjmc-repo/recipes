Hamburger Buns

Makes 8 buns · Bake 16 to 18 minutes at 375°F

Ingredients

Tangzhong

  • 2 tablespoons (27 g) water
  • 4 tablespoons (60 g) whole milk
  • 2 tablespoons (20 g) bread flour

Dough

  • 1/2 cup (120 g) whole milk, warmed to 95°F (30°C)
  • 1 tablespoon (9 g) instant yeast
  • 2 1/2 cups (320 g) bread flour
  • 1 teaspoon (7 g) fine sea salt
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons (35 g) granulated sugar
  • 1 whole egg
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 3 tablespoons (42 g) unsalted butter, softened

Egg wash and finish

  • 1 whole egg
  • Splash of whole milk
  • Melted butter, for brushing

Instructions

  1. In a pan over medium heat, combine the tangzhong water, milk and flour. Whisk until it thickens, then set aside.
  2. Warm 1/2 cup milk to 95°F (30°C), about 30 seconds in the microwave. In a mug, combine the warm milk with the instant yeast.
  3. In the mixer bowl, combine the flour, salt and sugar.
  4. With the mixer on low speed, add the yeast mixture. Wait a few seconds, then add the tangzhong, followed by the whole egg and the egg yolk.
  5. Mix on medium-low until thoroughly incorporated, then add the softened butter a little at a time.
  6. Knead the dough for 5 to 8 minutes.
  7. Shape the dough into a ball and place it in a greased bowl covered with a damp towel. Let it rise for 60 to 90 minutes, until doubled in size.
  8. Punch down the dough and divide it into 8 even pieces, about 85 g each.
  9. Shape each piece into a ball, transfer to a baking sheet, and cover with an inverted baking sheet. Let rise for 1 to 2 hours.
  10. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Whisk 1 egg with a splash of milk and brush it over the buns.
  11. Bake at 375°F for 16 to 18 minutes.
  12. Brush the hot buns with melted butter.
